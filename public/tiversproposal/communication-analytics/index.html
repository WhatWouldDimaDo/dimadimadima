01 · What my voice-recording prototype demonstrated

A recording archive can become a personal communication laboratory.

These are representative findings from my own voice recordings. They demonstrate the method and potential output—not a normative scorecard for Eric, his clients, or anyone else.

7.5 → 13.8hedges / 1K words

Context changes the voice

The prototype corpus showed substantially more hedging in meetings than solo notes. The useful insight is not “low confidence”; it is identifying which contexts alter an otherwise available, direct register.

12.1 → 37.5fillers / 1K words

Cognitive processing becomes audible

Meeting filler rate was more than three times the solo-note baseline. That creates a concrete practice target: structure the opening, shorten claim length, and substitute deliberate pauses.

0.796 → 0.580pitch-variation index

Performance mode compresses expression

Pitch variation was lower in meetings than solo speech. This can flag a context shift worth reviewing, but it cannot explain motive or diagnose state without human interpretation.

1,461recordings assessed

Longitudinal beats anecdotal

A large personal corpus made it possible to compare the same speaker across time, topic, audience, and setting instead of treating one awkward conversation as a stable trait.

The most useful output was a gap, not a grade.

The direct, expressive voice already existed in low-stakes contexts. The opportunity was to transfer more of that available register into meetings and evaluated conversations.

02 · Reusable framework

Local-first evidence, then context, then coaching.

The pipeline is intentionally sequential. Every downstream insight inherits the errors, permissions, and assumptions of the stage before it.

01
Capture

Use consented recordings from defined contexts; preserve date, source, speaker, and purpose.

02
Transcribe

Run consistent local transcription so comparative metrics are not distorted by mixed models.

03
Quality

Detect silence hallucinations, grade transcript quality, and diarize only where speaker attribution matters.

04
Measure

Extract linguistic, conversational, temporal, and acoustic features with transparent definitions.

05
Calibrate

Compare the person to their own baseline by audience, topic, time, and communication task.

06
Interpret

Use human review to distinguish behavior, context, device effects, and plausible alternative explanations.

07
Practice

Select one observable experiment, rehearse it, and reassess against the original context.

Corpus layer

Searchable transcripts, source lineage, quality grades, speaker and context metadata.

Measurement layer

Hedges, fillers, modals, questions, turns, pace, pauses, pitch variation, and longitudinal change.

Interpretation layer

Alternative explanations, context, personal goals, meaning, and coaching judgment.

Practice layer

One behavior, one context, a rehearsal protocol, and a follow-up comparison.

03 · Potential business concepts

Start as a high-touch service. Productize only what repeats.

The early advantage is not an app. It is a thoughtful facilitated interpretation that joins quantitative signals with Eric’s communication and ADHD coaching expertise.

01
Eric, executives, educators

Speaker rehearsal intelligence

Compare rehearsals and live talks for opening structure, pacing, filler clusters, question handling, and moments where energy rises or collapses.

02
ADHD professionals

Interview and executive communication lab

Surface long answers, sidebar triggers, buried outcomes, weak-modal patterns, and the gap between a confident private explanation and an evaluated conversation.

03
Coaches and facilitators

Coach reflection system

Review question-to-telling ratio, airtime balance, interruption, repair, client language adoption, and whether session openings and endings consistently produce direction.

04
Individuals and teams

Communication development tracker

Establish a personal baseline, select one behavioral experiment, and measure change by context over time—without ranking people against a generic “normal.”

06 · Non-negotiable guardrails

Insight without surveillance.

Explicit opt-in

No covert recording, passive employee scoring, or repurposing beyond the stated use.

Local-first processing

Raw sensitive audio stays on controlled hardware wherever practical.

No diagnosis

Do not claim to detect ADHD, mood, truthfulness, intent, or clinical state.

Context before inference

Device, room, audience, role, fatigue, and task can all change the signal.

Human review

Metrics prompt a conversation; they do not make consequential decisions.

Minimum retention

Keep only what the service requires; provide deletion and export paths.

The opportunity

Not “AI that judges how you speak.”
A private mirror that helps you choose what to practice.
