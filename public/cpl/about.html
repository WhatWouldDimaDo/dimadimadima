The studio

Three people, two aesthetics, one standard.

A&B is deliberately small. Every property gets the same three people — one who designs it, one who builds it, one who keeps it alive.

Arjun

Lead Designer

Arjun is an award-winning bonsai artist with a refined eye for balance, form and detail. His artistic background guides every plan toward landscapes that feel elegant, precise and intentional — never arbitrary.

He sits on the Board of the Atlanta Bonsai Society and teaches Keido, the traditional art of bonsai display, exhibiting his own trees annually. His practice spans Japanese maples, rock plantings and kusamono — the companion-planting tradition that pairs a tree with the smaller grasses and flowers around it.

A yard is a bonsai you walk through.

That discipline — restraint, proportion, patience — is what he brings to a half-acre lot as readily as a shallow pot. The scale changes. The eye doesn't.

from Arjun's exhibition practice

Blake

Hardscape & Operations

Blake specializes in hardscaping and structural elements — retaining walls, walkways, patios, the parts of a property that have to be right the first time. A background in home renovation brings a focus on craftsmanship and durability to every build. He's a Dunwoody local, and on most projects he's the person the neighbors actually see.

Gina

Landscape Designer

Gina has designed numerous high-end residential landscapes across Atlanta, with a thoughtful approach that enhances both beauty and environmental health. She thinks in layers — canopy, understory, groundcover — so a planting plan holds up in its second decade, not just its first season.

Shared philosophy

Two aesthetics, done extremely well

Clean & modern

Tight lines, restrained plant palettes, architecture allowed to lead. For properties that want the landscape to feel considered, not decorative.

Natural & layered

Looser massing, seasonal texture, a garden that looks like it arrived over time. For properties that want to feel settled from day one.
